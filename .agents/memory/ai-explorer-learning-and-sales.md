---
name: Learn Think Build learning and sales scope
description: Product goals for children's AI literacy and parent-led learning at home.
---

Learn Think Build is a parent-guided, at-home AI discovery app for curious learners 10+, with age paths for 10–12, 13–15, and 16+. Its purpose is to help children explore AI safely, grow practical skills gradually, and choose making over passive screen time. Keep it playful and parent-trusted, not school-sales or job-training marketing. The family price is $4.99/month and remains “coming soon” until billing is enabled. Testimonials must be genuine family statements published only with permission; do not invent reviews. Keep guardian-owned accounts, learner display name and age band only, simulated AI examples, and no open-ended live learner chatbot.

**Why:** The user wants child-centered, at-home AI exploration with guardian involvement, safe creation, and truthful public claims.

**How to apply:** Preserve the four 20-session chapters, all-mode lesson loop, age-banded scenarios, retrieval, reflections, server-side checks, chapter projects, and guardian approval. Each learner gets no more than ten active minutes in a rolling 24-hour window, without streak pressure. Keep the leaderboard private to each guardian-owned family; award points for completed missions, best quiz scores, mastery, and best review only—not time or streaks. Schoolwork may appear as a responsible-use scenario, but school selling is out of scope.
