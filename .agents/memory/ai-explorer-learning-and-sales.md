Learn Think Build is a parent-focused, at-home introduction to AI for learners starting at age 10, with recommended paths for ages 10–12, 13–15, and 16+. The year-one learning goal is for a learner to build and explain a basic app. Advancement should be based on demonstrated understanding and passing assessments, not lesson completion alone. Teach prompt writing, checking AI outputs, appropriate use, privacy, and academic integrity—including when AI use is cheating. Each lesson should integrate narration with visuals, interaction, retrieval, reflection, and spaced review; do not match learners to a supposed visual or auditory learning style. Do not add an open-ended live AI chatbot for learners; simulate AI examples in lessons. Real AI tools should only be introduced with strict parent or adult supervision. Planned family pricing is $9.99 per month. Do not position the product for school sales or add school licensing workflows. Do not connect Stripe or enable billing until the LLC details are ready; the goal in the meantime is to get the app URL live, with checkout clearly marked as coming soon. Public testimonials must be genuine family statements approved for publication; never invent reviews.

**Why:** The user wants Learn Think Build marketed to parents for at-home AI introduction, not school sales; they selected age bands 10–12, 13–15, and 16+, and deferred Stripe until LLC details are ready.

**How to apply:** Market the product to parents and guardians for guided learning at home. Use integrated learning modes in every lesson rather than learning-style matching. Keep classroom/teacher rules only where they support responsible-use lessons, not as a school-sales pitch. Use authentic, permissioned family testimonials only. Preserve the distinction between planned curriculum/tool access and features currently implemented. Keep internal artifact and billing identifiers stable unless they are intentionally migrated. Keep the learning app independent from Stripe until the LLC details are ready.
