name: Learn Think Build learning and sales scope
description: Product goals for the children's AI literacy curriculum and school licensing.
---

Learn Think Build is for learners starting at age 10. The year-one learning goal is for a learner to build and explain a basic app. Advancement should be based on demonstrated understanding and passing assessments, not lesson completion alone. Teach prompt writing, checking AI outputs, appropriate use, privacy, and academic integrity—including when AI use is cheating. Real AI tools should be introduced later with strict adult or teacher supervision; do not present unsupervised AI chat as a child-safe feature. Family pricing is $79.99 upfront per year. School licensing is intended to be annual per student, but teacher/classroom and school purchasing workflows are not yet available.

**Why:** These are the user's product goals and rollout constraints; the user selected Learn Think Build as the new product name.

**How to apply:** Use Learn Think Build in public-facing branding. Preserve the distinction between planned curriculum/tool access and features currently implemented. Keep school pricing and classroom administration explicitly marked as unavailable until those workflows exist. Keep internal artifact and billing identifiers stable unless they are intentionally migrated.
